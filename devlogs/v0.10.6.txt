Beast and Crimson — v0.10.6 Devlog

Date: 2026-11-25

---

Voice Lines for All 30 Warriors

Every playable warrior across Bloodfang, Guardvaw, and Goldclaw now has two unique deployment voice clips (60 clips total) wired into the battle audio system. Deploying a unit alternates between its lines, cuts off any previous overlapping voice, respects your Effects/Master volume settings, and stops cleanly on pause, retreat, or tab switch.

---

Clearer Reward & Runic Burn Equipping

Earning new Runic Burns and warriors is now much easier to act on between battles:

- Post-battle Runic Burn and achievement cards now include a direct "Equip in Warband" button that jumps straight to an unequipped lineup warrior, resets any active stat filter, and scrolls to highlight the new burn.

- The Campaign screen's Warband button shows a badge when you have unbound Runic Burns ready for your active lineup or newly unlocked warriors.

- The 5-slot Battle Lineup strip in the Warband now displays burn dots (and empty dashed rings for unequipped slots) so you can see at a glance who still needs a Runic Burn.

---

UI & Visual Polish

- Fixed a styling issue on the stage briefing popup where the artwork's bottom border cut across the stage title like a strikethrough.

- Recent 0.10.x polish also includes non-intrusive hover/focus tooltips across the HUD and Warband stats, foreground lane lamp posts with warm glow, stronghold hit-flash improvements, and a rebalanced enemy opening vanguard and per-stage pace curve.

Available on itch.io (Web HTML5 + Linux AppImage) and GitHub Releases (.AppImage, .deb, .rpm).
