How to play & controls

Spend gold to deploy warriors. They advance and fight on their own. Cast their abilities when ready, and raze the keep before your Totem falls.

1–5
Deploy a warrior
Q / W / E / A / S
Cast a slot's ability
F
Cast any ready ability
R
Call your Totem spell
X
Switch 1× / 2× speed
Space / Esc
Pause or resume
Tab
Move between controls

On touch screens, use the portraits, Cast buttons, and Totem button. Auto-cast also uses your Totem spell.
